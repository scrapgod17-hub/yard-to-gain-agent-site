Yard to Gain Agent

A private production tool that helps the Yard to Gain team research, create, schedule, and manage original football videos on its own YouTube channel.

How Google data is used

The tool uses Google OAuth only after the channel owner approves access. It reads the channel's recent uploads to avoid duplicate topics and uploads or schedules videos the owner has approved. It is not offered to the public and does not sell or share Google user data.
