Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Yard to Gain Agent is a private content-production tool used by the owner of the Yard to Gain YouTube channel. It is not offered as a public service.

Google user data

After the channel owner grants permission through Google OAuth, the tool may access basic YouTube channel information and recent upload metadata, and may upload or schedule videos on that channel. This access is used only to operate the Yard to Gain publishing workflow, avoid duplicate topics, and manage owner-approved videos.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are used by the private publishing workflow. Google user data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with third parties. The tool does not download subscriber identities, private messages, or viewers' personal information.

Retention and deletion

Recent video titles and identifiers may appear temporarily in workflow logs or production records. The channel owner can revoke access at any time through their Google Account permissions and can request deletion of retained data by contacting the address below.
